Archive overview

Some truths are best left unwritten.

ERASURE is a narrative mystery set within The Unwritten, a larger story universe. This public archive offers fragments of the setting and premise—without revealing the answers meant to be discovered in the game.

Record 001 · Location

Virex City

A near-future metropolis shaped by interconnected systems, persistent records, and the belief that information can tell the whole truth. Its streets have histories. So do the people moving through them.

The public archive does not yet reveal how the city's systems connect to the mystery.
